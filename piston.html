Four-Piston Engine Assembly

Mechanical Design, Assembly and Motion Simulation

Project Overview

This project involved the 3D CAD modelling and assembly of a four-piston engine mechanism using SolidWorks. The assembly consists of four pistons connected to a rotating crankshaft, demonstrating the conversion of rotary motion into reciprocating motion.

Design and Assembly

The engine components were assembled to represent their mechanical relationships and movement. An exploded view was prepared to illustrate the arrangement and positioning of the components.

View Piston Exploded-View Drawing (PDF)

Motion Simulation

A SolidWorks motion study was used to simulate the rotation of the crankshaft and the resulting reciprocating movement of the four pistons. The simulation demonstrates the relationship between crankshaft rotation and piston movement.
