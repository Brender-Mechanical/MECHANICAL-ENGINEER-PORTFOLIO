Excavator Assembly

3D CAD Modelling, Engineering Drawing and Motion Study

Project Overview

This project involved the 3D CAD modelling and assembly of an excavator using SolidWorks. The model represents the major structural and mechanical components of an excavator and demonstrates their arrangement within the complete assembly.

Engineering Drawing

The engineering drawing presents the excavator design and the arrangement of its components. It provides a technical reference for understanding the geometry and structure of the assembly.

View Excavator Engineering Drawing (PDF)

Motion Study

A SolidWorks motion study was created to demonstrate the movement of the excavator assembly. The video showcases the simulated motion of the model and illustrates the interaction between its components.

Skills Demonstrated

  • 3D CAD modelling using SolidWorks
  • Mechanical assembly and component relationships
  • Engineering drawing interpretation
  • Motion study and simulation
  • Mechanical design visualisation
