Hydraulic Cylinder Assembly

3D CAD Modelling, Exploded View and Motion Simulation

Project Overview

This project involved the 3D CAD modelling and assembly of a hydraulic cylinder mechanism using SolidWorks. The project explores the arrangement of mechanical components and the movement associated with cylinder operation.

Exploded View

An exploded view was created to illustrate the individual components of the assembly and their relative positions. This helps demonstrate how the components fit together to form the complete mechanism.

Motion Simulation

A SolidWorks motion simulation was created to demonstrate the rotational movement and the pumping or extension motion represented by the model. The simulation helps illustrate the relationship between component movement and the operation of the assembly.

Engineering Drawings

The engineering drawings provide technical views of the hydraulic cylinder design and communicate the geometry and arrangement of the components.
