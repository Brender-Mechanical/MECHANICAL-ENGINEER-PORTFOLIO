Scissor Lift Mechanism

3D CAD Modelling, Mechanical Assembly and Motion Simulation

Project Overview

This project involved the 3D CAD modelling and assembly of a scissor lift mechanism using SolidWorks. The design demonstrates how a scissor linkage system can be used to produce controlled vertical lifting and lowering motion.

Design and Assembly

The mechanical components were assembled to represent the structure and movement of the scissor lift. An exploded view and a complete structural drawing were prepared to illustrate the arrangement of the components and their relationships within the assembly.

View Scissor Lift Drawings (PDF)

Motion Simulation

A motion simulation was created in SolidWorks to demonstrate the movement of the scissor lift mechanism. The simulation illustrates the lifting and lowering motion and the rotational movement of the mechanism during operation.

Skills Demonstrated

  • 3D CAD modelling using SolidWorks
  • Mechanical assembly and component relationships
  • Exploded-view and engineering drawing interpretation
  • Motion study and kinematic simulation
  • Understanding of linkage mechanisms and linear motion
