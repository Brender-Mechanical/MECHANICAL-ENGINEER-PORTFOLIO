Bobcat Assembly

3D CAD Modelling, Exploded View and Motion Study

Project Overview

This project involved the 3D CAD modelling and assembly of a Bobcat mechanism using SolidWorks. The project demonstrates the arrangement of mechanical components, their assembly relationships, and the movement of the model during a motion study.

Engineering Drawing

The engineering drawing presents the design and structural arrangement of the Bobcat assembly. It provides a technical reference for understanding the geometry and configuration of the model.

View Bobcat Engineering Drawing (PDF)

Exploded View and Motion Study

The SolidWorks motion study demonstrates the exploded view of the assembly, the collapsing and reassembly sequence, and the rotational movement shown in the simulation video.

Skills Demonstrated

  • 3D CAD modelling using SolidWorks
  • Mechanical assembly and component relationships
  • Exploded-view representation
  • Motion study and animation
  • Engineering drawing interpretation
